Rooted like a baobab

Strength, resilience and community, the way the baobab stands on our island.

LBT began in 2023 as an informal group of young people from Likoma Island and was formally registered in 2025. Likoma has great potential in culture, tourism and youth-driven development, but young people face limited opportunities, unemployment, poor access to education and low awareness of health and environmental issues. We answer with performing arts, cultural activities and digital innovation.

Our vision

A transformed and empowered youth community that uses arts and innovation to drive positive social, cultural and economic change in Malawi.

Our mission

To harness the power of performing arts, culture and innovation as platforms for youth empowerment, behavioural change and community development on Likoma Island and beyond.

  • Youth leadership
  • Creativity and innovation
  • Inclusiveness for all
  • Accountability and transparency
  • Sustainability

What we do

Seven focus areas, one approach: put young people on stage and in charge.

Health

Awareness on HIV/AIDS, mental health, sexual and reproductive health and substance abuse prevention through theatre and campaigns.

Education

Literacy support, theatre-for-education, debates and school-based clubs.

Entrepreneurship

Building youth capacity in business, creative industries and self-reliance.

Environment

Climate change awareness, tree planting and waste management through the arts.

AI and digital skills

Technology, coding and digital literacy so youth can thrive in the digital economy.

Tourism and culture

Showcasing Likoma’s heritage, promoting local tourism and preserving traditions through festivals and performances.

Trending issues

Youth dialogue on governance, democracy and emerging social issues, on stage and online.

Key activities

  • Community theatre and cultural performances on social issues
  • Awareness campaigns on health, environment and education
  • Training workshops on arts, entrepreneurship and digital literacy
  • Cultural festivals and exhibitions promoting Likoma’s heritage
  • Youth clubs in schools to encourage creativity and leadership
  • Collaboration with government, NGOs and cultural institutions

Our island

Likoma sits in Lake Malawi, with sandy beaches, fishing boats and green hills. This is the home we perform for.

Open membership

Anyone aged 15 and above who supports our objectives can join. Young people aged 15 to 35 hold the majority of leadership roles, and supportive adults can join as patrons or advisors.

Members decide

Our General Assembly of all members is the supreme decision-making body. It approves policies, plans, budgets and annual reports, and meets once a year.

Specialist sub-committees

Health, Environment, Arts, Entrepreneurship, Digital Skills and Tourism.
